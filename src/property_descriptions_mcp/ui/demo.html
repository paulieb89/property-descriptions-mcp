How it works

Step 1
Provide an address and postcode. Optionally paste a Rightmove URL to pull in the listing's photos.
Step 2
Your AI fetches comparable sales, EPC rating, current listings, and location context — and reads the property images to describe rooms accurately.
Step 3
Receive three description variants (Professional, Luxury, Family-friendly) plus portal-ready formats: Rightmove summary, social caption, email subject, and feature bullets.
